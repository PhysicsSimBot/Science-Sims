📐 Alberta Physics 20/30 Formula & Data Sheet
Physics Constants & Conversions
Acceleration due to gravity (Earth): g = 9.81 m/s²
Newton definition: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²
Joule definition: 1 J = 1 N·m = 1 kg·m²/s²
Speed conversion: 1 km/h = 1/3.6 m/s
1. Kinematics Formulas
Uniform velocity: vave = Δd/Δt
Uniform acceleration: a = (vf − vi)/t
Displacement (with a): d = vit + ½at²
Displacement (with v_f): d = vft − ½at²
Displacement (average v): d = (vi + vf/2) t
Timeless kinematic: vf² = vi² + 2ad
2. Vectors & Trigonometry
Horizontal component: Rx = R cos θ
Vertical component: Ry = R sin θ
Pythagorean magnitude: R = √(Rx² + Ry²)
Resultant angle: tan θ = Ry/Rx
Sine Law: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C
Cosine Law: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C
3. Dynamics (Forces) Formulas
Newton's Second Law: Fnet = ma = ΣF
Gravitational Force (Weight): Fg = mg
Friction force: Ff = μFN
Incline parallel force: F∥ = mg sin θ
Incline normal force: FN = mg cos θ
4. Work & Energy Formulas
Work: W = Fd cos θ = ΔE Gravitational Potential: Ep = mgh
Kinetic Energy: Ek = ½mv² Elastic Potential Energy: Es = ½kx²
Total Mechanical Energy: Em = Ep + Ek + Es Conservation / Non-isolated: Em,i − Wf = Em,f
Alberta Diploma Significant Digits Rules

• Multiplication / Division: Round your final answer to the least number of significant digits in the given values.
• Leading zeros (e.g. 0.0050) are not significant (0.0050 has 2 sig figs).
• Trailing zeros with a decimal point (e.g. 50.0 or 50.) are significant (50.0 has 3 sig figs, 50. has 2 sig figs).
• Scientific Notation: Format numbers like 1.8e3 or 1.8x10^3 (mantissa dictates significant digits).
