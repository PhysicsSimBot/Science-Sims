Unit A: Momentum and Impulse Curriculum Summary

1. Vector Momentum & Impulse

• p⃗ = mv⃗ (units: kg·m/s or N·s)
• Impulse: J⃗ = Δp⃗ = F⃗netΔt = mv⃗f − mv⃗i
• In an isolated system (no external net forces): Σp⃗i = Σp⃗f

2. Elasticity & Kinetic Energy

• Ek = ½mv2 (scalar, Joules)
• Elastic (e = 1.0): ΣEki = ΣEkf
• Inelastic (0 < e < 1): ΣEki > ΣEkf (ΔEk converted to thermal/sound)
• Completely Inelastic (e = 0): Objects stick together (maximum Ek loss)

3. 2D Vector Component Method

• Σpix = Σpfx &implies; Σ(mivix) = Σ(mfvfx)
• Σpiy = Σpfy &implies; Σ(miviy) = Σ(mfvfy)
• ptotal = √[(Σpx)2 + (Σpy)2]
• θ = tan−1(Σpy / Σpx)

4. Alberta Compass Conventions

• Quadrant 1: [θ° N of E] or [(90 − θ)° E of N]
• Quadrant 2: [θ° N of W] or [(90 − θ)° W of N]
• Quadrant 3: [θ° S of W] or [(90 − θ)° W of S]
• Quadrant 4: [θ° S of E] or [(90 − θ)° E of S]
