📋 Kinematics Graph Rules Summary (For Your Physics 20 Notes)
Graph Type What the Y-Value Means What the Slope Means (RiseRun) What the Area Means
Distance vs. Time (d vs t) Total odometer distance travelled (≥ 0). Speed (v)
Slope can never be negative!		 No physical meaning
Position vs. Time (d vs t) Location relative to origin (0 m). Can be +, 0, or −. Velocity (v)
Positive slope = Forward (+), Negative slope = Backward (−).		 No physical meaning
Velocity vs. Time (v vs t) Speed and direction (+ or −). Crossing zero = turning around. Acceleration (a)
a = ΔvΔt (Flat line = 0 m/s2)		 Displacement (Δd)
Above axis is +, below axis is −.
Speed vs. Time (v vs t) Magnitude of velocity (|v| ≥ 0). Never below axis. Rate of speed change. Total Distance (d)
Sum of all geometric areas.
